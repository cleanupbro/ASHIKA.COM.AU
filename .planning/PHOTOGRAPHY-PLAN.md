# ASHIKA — Product Photography & AI Image Plan

> Sam has the physical garments. No studio needed.

---

## Step 1: Take the Photos (30 min per garment)

### Setup
- **Surface:** White bedsheet on floor or large table, ironed flat
- **Light:** Near a large window, 10am-2pm. Diffuse harsh sun with a thin white sheet/curtain
- **Phone:** iPhone, MAX resolution. Settings > Camera > "Most Compatible" (JPG not HEIC)
- **Angle:** Shoot from directly above (stand on chair/stool for flat-lays)

### 4 Shots Per Garment (minimum)

| # | Shot | How | Why |
|---|------|-----|-----|
| 1 | Front flat-lay | Spread garment fully, symmetrical, no hidden folds. Shoot from above. | Primary product image |
| 2 | Back flat-lay | Flip it, same position | Shows back design/blouse |
| 3 | Detail close-up | Zoom in on embroidery, zari, border pattern, fabric texture | Sells the quality |
| 4 | Draped/styled | Hang on wooden/padded hanger against plain wall, or drape over chair | Shows how it falls |

### Tips
- Steam/iron garments first (wrinkles kill premium feel)
- Remove ALL tags, stickers, lint, threads
- Keep your shadow out of the shot
- Take 2-3 of each angle, pick best later
- For lehengas: photograph skirt + top + dupatta separately AND together

### Naming
`{category}_{color}_{name}_{angle}.jpg`

Examples:
- `saree_red_kanjeevaram_front.jpg`
- `lehenga_maroon_bridal_detail.jpg`
- `sherwani_ivory_wedding_hanger.jpg`

## Step 2: AI Enhancement Pipeline

### 2A. Background Removal + Cleanup
**Tool:** Gemini (Comet browser) or Remove.bg (free: 50 images/month)
- Upload each photo
- Remove background to pure white (#FFFFFF)
- Fix any color cast from window lighting

### 2B. Product Photo Enhancement
**Tool:** GPT-4o (via Comet > ChatGPT)
- Upload cleaned photo
- Prompt: "Enhance this product photo for a luxury fashion e-commerce catalog. Clean white background, professional studio lighting, accurate colors, sharp fabric texture details. High-end quality like glamcorner.com.au."
- This corrects lighting, sharpens details, balances colors
- DO NOT ask it to change the garment — enhance only

### 2C. AI Model Shots (The Money Shot)
**Tool:** GPT-4o (best accuracy) or Gemini (free + fast)

**Upload your flat-lay photo + prompt:**

For sarees:
> "Generate a professional fashion catalog photo of a South Asian woman wearing this exact saree. She is standing in a clean photography studio with soft diffused lighting. Full body shot, elegant traditional drape, natural pose. The saree details — fabric, color, embroidery, border pattern — must match the uploaded photo exactly. High-end e-commerce quality."

For lehengas:
> "Generate a professional fashion catalog photo of a South Asian woman wearing this exact lehenga set. Studio setting, full body, bridal/festive pose. The lehenga skirt, top/choli, and dupatta details must match the uploaded photo. GlamCorner-level quality."

For sherwanis:
> "Generate a professional fashion catalog photo of a South Asian man wearing this exact sherwani. Studio setting, full body, confident traditional pose. Fabric, color, and embroidery details must match the uploaded photo."

**Compare results from GPT-4o vs Gemini — pick the best per garment.**

### 2D. Lifestyle/Scene Shots (For Hero + Marketing)
**Tool:** Grok (via Comet > grok.x.ai, free with X Premium)

Prompts:
- "South Asian woman in a red Kanjeevaram saree at an outdoor Indian wedding. Golden hour, fairy lights bokeh, editorial fashion photography."
- "Group of women in colorful lehengas at a Diwali celebration. Warm lighting, vibrant colors, candid joy."
- "Close-up of gold zari embroidery on silk fabric, macro photography, shallow depth of field."

These are for: hero section, category banners, Instagram/social media, email headers.

### 2E. Hero Video
**Tool:** Grok or Gemini video generation

Prompt: "Cinematic slow-motion montage of Indian ethnic wear. Silk sarees flowing in gentle breeze, gold embroidery macro details, South Asian woman getting ready for a wedding adjusting jewelry. Warm golden lighting, shallow depth of field. 10 seconds, seamless loop, no text overlays."

## Step 3: Image Processing

### Format
- Convert all final images to **WebP** (Next.js Image component handles this automatically)
- Product images: 1200x1600px (3:4 ratio, portrait)
- Thumbnails: auto-generated by next/image
- Lifestyle/hero: 1920x1080px (16:9)

### Upload to Supabase Storage
- Bucket: `product-images` (public access)
- Structure: `product-images/{product_id}/front.webp`, `model.webp`, `detail.webp`, `back.webp`
- Update product records in DB with Supabase Storage URLs

## Step 4: Per-Product Image Set (Target)

Each product should end up with:

| # | Image | Source | Display Location |
|---|-------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Front flat-lay (enhanced) | Real photo + AI cleanup | Product grid thumbnail, detail page main image |
| 2 | Back flat-lay (enhanced) | Real photo + AI cleanup | Detail page gallery |
| 3 | Detail close-up | Real photo (minimal editing) | Detail page gallery |
| 4 | Model wearing garment | AI-generated (GPT-4o/Gemini) | Detail page gallery, marketing |
| 5 | Lifestyle/scene (optional) | AI-generated (Grok) | Category banner, social media |

## Which AI Tool for What (Quick Reference)

| Task | Best Tool | Access |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Background removal | Gemini or Remove.bg | Free |
| Photo enhancement (lighting, color) | GPT-4o | Comet > ChatGPT (Plus) |
| Model wearing your garment | GPT-4o (most accurate) | Comet > ChatGPT |
| Quick model shots (volume) | Gemini | Comet > Gemini (free) |
| Lifestyle/scene shots | Grok | Comet > grok.x.ai (X Premium) |
| Hero video | Grok or Gemini video gen | Comet |

---

## Rules
- Product grid images = ALWAYS real enhanced photos (not AI-generated)
- Model shots = secondary images in detail gallery
- Never misrepresent AI images as photos of the actual garment
- Generate 2-3 options per model shot, pick the best
- Hero/lifestyle images are clearly marketing — OK to be fully AI-generated
